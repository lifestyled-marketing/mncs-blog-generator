# Messaging Guide - Emberly Counseling (EC)

## TAGLINE

Evidence-Based Therapy with a Human Touch

## MISSION

To create a warm, person-centered space where teens and adults in Pennsylvania can safely process trauma, OCD, anxiety, depression, relationship strain, and perinatal challenges, and leave with practical tools, renewed self-trust, and lasting capacity for life.

## Why Choose Us?

* **Trauma-informed:** EMDR-trained; safety and pacing come first.
* **Relational and down-to-earth:** You're met exactly where you are, no jargon, no judgment.
* **Specialized focus:** OCD, trauma, anxiety/depression, relationship patterns, pregnancy & postpartum seasons.
* **Tools and practical tips:** You'll leave sessions with skills you can use between sessions, not just insights.

## Enemies

* **Shame & Silence:** the idea that you must be "fine" and go through hard things alone.
* **The Quick-Fix Myth:** shallow mental-health hacks and "toxic positivity" that minimize real pain and keep people stuck.
* **One-Size-Fits-All Therapy:** generic advice that ignores evidence. We use approaches with strong support like EMDR for trauma and Internal Family Systems and Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for OCD and anxiety.

## Audience

### PAIN

* Intrusive thoughts or compulsions disrupting work, parenting, or routines
* Panic, constant worry, and decision fatigue
* Low mood, loss of interest, sleep and energy changes
* Conflict cycles, unclear boundaries, isolation, or people-pleasing
* Pregnancy and postpartum challenges including scary thoughts, guilt, identity shifts, or birth trauma
* Rumination and reassurance seeking that take excessive amounts of time and attention
* Avoiding triggers, canceling plans, shrinking activities
* Feeling numb or overwhelmed and struggling to follow through
* Outbursts behaviors and dysregulated stress responses

### DESIRE

* To feel safe in my own skin; fewer spirals, more capacity.
* Tools that help me practically work toward my goals between sessions.
* A therapist who "gets it" and doesn't rush me.
* More ease in relationships and clearer boundaries.
* To enjoy parenthood/life without the constant alarm bells.

## Lexicon

### CORE PHRASES

* Safe, seen, supported
* Gentle and clear
* We meet you where you are
* Practical tools you can use between sessions
* Evidence based
* tailored to you
* Small steps
* Your pace with a plan
* Steady support for real life

### PHRASES WE USE

* Plain language that is warm and direct
* "You" and "we" to signal partnership
* Present tense and clear commitments
* Action verbs like practice, build, learn, unhook, re engage
* Specific names for concerns when helpful
* Concrete outcomes like sleep, focus, boundaries, routines
* Evidence based references kept simple and clear

### PHRASES WE AVOID

* Quick fixes, hacks, cures, miracle claims
* Absolutes or guarantees
* Jargon or trendy buzzwords
* Shaming or pathologizing labels
* Vague promises like transform your life instantly
* Cold, overly clinical tone
* Metaphors that minimize real pain

## Visual Difference

* **Warm neutrals**, one ember accent, generous white space.
* **Calm contrast** that lets the words do the work.
* **Open layouts**, simple hierarchy, one idea per block.
* **Human touch** is felt. Not staged. Plain-language labels.
* **Gentle indicators** for next step, what to expect. Clarity even in design. Clear privacy cues
* **Human-clinician-led.** Headline first, design supports. Sentence case, plain punctuation.
* **Steady motion.** Small, unhurried transitions. No bounce or spectacle.
* **Accessible by default.** Readable sizes, real contrast, inclusive imagery.
* **Avoiding:** Therapy tropes, pastel clutter, busy gradients, tiny gray text.
